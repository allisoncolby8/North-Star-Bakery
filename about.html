About North Star Bakery

Our story

North Star Bakery started with three recipes, a borrowed mixer, and a promise to make food we would be proud to share with our own neighbors. Today, the menu has grown, but the rhythm is the same: start early, work carefully, and leave room for conversation.

Our sourcing philosophy

We look for flour from thoughtful mills, dairy from nearby farms, eggs from responsible producers, and fruit from growers we can name. We choose ingredients for flavor first and build recipes that let them shine.

When the fruit changes, the danish changes. Following the seasons keeps our work surprising and our ingredients at their best.
